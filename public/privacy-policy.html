Information We Collect

Novel Era collects minimal data necessary to provide our reading services. We are transparent about what we collect and why.

Data Collection Summary

We collect data to: Provide reading services, sync progress across devices, and improve app functionality. We do not: Sell personal data, track reading content, or share data with third parties for advertising.

Account Information

  • Email Address: For account creation and authentication
  • Username: For personalization (optional)
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted login credentials

Reading Data

  • Reading Progress: Bookmarks, chapter progress, reading position
  • Library Data: Saved novels, favorites, reading lists
  • Preferences: Theme settings, font preferences, TTS settings
  • Download Data: Offline content for reading

Technical Information

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version for compatibility
  • App Analytics: Crash reports, performance metrics (anonymized)
  • Usage Statistics: Feature usage for app improvement (anonymized)

Data Sharing & Third Parties

Novel Era does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Service Providers We Use

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and hosting services
  • Google Play Services: App distribution and crash reporting

When We Might Share Data

  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or court order
  • Safety Concerns: To protect users or prevent illegal activities
  • Business Transfer: In case of company merger or acquisition (with user notification)

Third-Party Content Sources

Novel Era aggregates content from various online sources. When you access content, you may be subject to those sources' privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies if privacy is a concern.

Data Security & Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL
  • Secure Storage: Data stored on Google Firebase with enterprise-grade security
  • Authentication: Secure login with password hashing and optional 2FA
  • Regular Updates: Security patches and updates applied regularly
  • Limited Access: Only authorized personnel have access to systems

Your Security Responsibilities

• Use a strong, unique password for your account
• Keep your app updated to the latest version
• Log out from shared devices
• Report any suspicious activity immediately

Your Privacy Rights

You have complete control over your data. Here are your rights and how to exercise them:

Data Rights (GDPR, CCPA Compliant)

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we have about you
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request complete removal of your account and data
  • Portability: Download your data in a standard format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing

How to Exercise Your Rights

Via Mobile App:

  • Go to Settings → Privacy → Data Management
  • Choose "Delete Account" for instant removal
  • Export your data before deletion if needed

Via Email:

  • Contact: hindevstudios@gmail.com
  • Include: Your email address and specific request
  • Response time: Within 72 hours for deletion, 30 days for other requests

Instant Account Deletion

Novel Era offers instant account deletion directly from the mobile app. No waiting periods, no email verification needed. Your data is permanently removed immediately upon confirmation.
