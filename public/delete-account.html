🗑️ Delete Your Account and Data

You have the right to delete your Novel Era account and all associated personal data. This page provides clear instructions on how to request account deletion as required by Google Play Store policies.

Steps to Request Account Deletion:

1. Click the "Send Deletion Request" button below
2. Your email client will open with a pre-filled message
3. Add your account details (email or user ID associated with your Novel Era account)
4. Send the email to our support team
5. We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation

⚠️ Important Information

Data Retention Policy:

  • All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of your request

  • After the retention period, no data is kept in any form
  • This action is irreversible - you cannot recover your account or data after deletion
