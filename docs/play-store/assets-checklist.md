# Assets Checklist

## Required Play Store Visuals

- App icon: 512 x 512 PNG, uploaded in Play Console
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 JPG or PNG
- Phone screenshots: at least 2, recommended 4 to 8

## Recommended Screenshot Set

Capture from a release or production-like Android build.

1. Onboarding hero screen
   - Shows premium visual quality and brand tone
2. Search screen
   - Shows source search and discovery
3. Reader screen
   - Shows immersive typography and theme customization
4. TTS controls
   - Shows listening capability
5. Favorites or recents
   - Shows continuity and organization
6. Account/backup settings
   - Shows optional sync and account support
7. EPUB or PDF export flow
   - Shows library/export utility

## Screenshot Caption Ideas

- `Search across your favorite novel sources`
- `Read in a clean, immersive reader`
- `Tune typography, themes, and spacing`
- `Listen with built-in text-to-speech controls`
- `Track favorites and reading progress`
- `Keep preferences backed up with optional sign-in`

## Brand Notes

- The current brand tone is elegant, literary, and premium
- Use dark, atmospheric screenshots that match the in-app look
- Avoid showing unfinished trial or premium messaging unless that flow is fully implemented

## Final Asset Pass

- Remove debug indicators
- Use real production-safe content
- Avoid copyrighted promotional images you do not own
- Make sure screenshots reflect the exact features described in `listing-copy.md`
