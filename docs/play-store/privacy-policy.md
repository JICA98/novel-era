# Privacy Policy for Atelier

Last updated: `2026-04-11`

Atelier respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

Atelier may process the following information:

- Account information, such as your email address, when you sign in with email or Google
- A user identifier provided by Firebase Authentication
- App preferences, such as reading theme, typography, text-to-speech settings, and preferred sources
- Optional profile details you choose to save, such as display name or tagline
- Reading-related data, such as favorites and chapter progress
- Backup metadata, such as device model name and timestamps, when cloud backup is used

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Sign you in and manage your account session
- Save your preferences across sessions
- Back up and restore your settings and reading-tracker data
- Provide reader customization, search, export, and other app features
- Maintain and improve app reliability

## Cloud Services

Atelier uses Firebase services to support authentication and optional cloud backup. When you choose to sign in and use backup-related features, your account-linked data may be stored on Firebase infrastructure.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may rely on service providers that process data on our behalf to operate the app, including authentication and cloud database providers.

## Your Choices

You can use parts of the app without signing in.

If you sign in, you may be able to:

- Sign out at any time
- Stop using cloud backup features
- Request deletion of remote account-linked data by contacting us

## Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide the app's features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Backup data associated with your account may remain stored until deleted or overwritten.

## Children

Atelier is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards and rely on industry-standard transport security offered by our service providers, but no system is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the date above when changes are made.
