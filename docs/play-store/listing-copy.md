## Full Description

Recommended:

`Atelier is a polished reading app built for novel fans who want a calmer, richer reading experience.

Search across supported sources, open chapters quickly, and settle into a customizable reader with themes, typography controls, and distraction-free layouts. Keep track of favorites, recent reads, and chapter progress, then export supported content for your own library workflows.

With Atelier, you can:

- Search and browse supported novel sources
- Read chapters in a clean, immersive reader
- Customize font, spacing, alignment, padding, and reading themes
- Use built-in text-to-speech controls for hands-free listening
- Track reading progress, favorites, and recent activity
- Back up preferences and trackers with an optional account
- Sign in with email or Google
- Export supported content to EPUB and PDF
- Keep your reading environment tailored to your style

Atelier is designed for readers who care about atmosphere, comfort, and continuity across sessions.

Notes:
- Some features depend on supported sources and network availability.
- Account-based backup is optional.
- Export and sync availability may vary by content source and app version.`
