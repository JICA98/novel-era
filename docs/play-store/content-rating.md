# Content Rating Guidance

This is a draft for the Play Console content rating questionnaire based on the current app behavior.

## Likely Rating Direction

Likely target:

`Teen` or equivalent, pending the actual source catalog shown inside the app

Reason:

- The app appears to aggregate and display third-party novel content
- The rating depends heavily on the most mature content users can access through supported sources

## Key Questions To Review Carefully

- Does the app display or link to sexually suggestive or explicit written content?
- Does the app display graphic violence, self-harm, abuse, or horror content from indexed novels?
- Does the app include unrestricted browsing of mature third-party content?
- Can users search the wider catalog freely without age gating?

## Safe Default Approach

- Rate based on the highest maturity level reasonably reachable in the app, not only the home screen
- If the app can surface mature novels from remote sources, do not rate it as broadly child-safe without filtering

## Suggested Answer Framing

- User-generated content: `No`, unless users can upload reviews/comments/content
- Online content: `Yes`, because the app fetches remote novel content
- Content filtering: only claim filtering if it is truly implemented and enforced

## Recommendation

Before release, define one of these product positions:

1. General audience reader with only curated age-safe sources
2. Teen-plus reader with wider catalog access
3. Mature-content reader with explicit gating and policy review

If the app does not actively restrict mature source material, plan for a stricter rating.
