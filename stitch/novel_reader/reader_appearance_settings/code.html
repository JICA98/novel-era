Chapter 14: The Whispering Gallery

The corridor stretched out before Elara, a cavernous throat of obsidian and silver. Every step she took echoed with a clarity that felt invasive, as if the very walls were leaning in to catch the rhythm of her heartbeat.

In the "Literary Atelier" of the high mages, silence was not merely the absence of sound; it was a physical weight, a velvet curtain draped over the consciousness. She remembered her father's warning about the Digital Curator—a ghost in the machine that sorted the memories of the world as if they were fine art.

The light novels of the Old World were preserved here, their prose breathing through the interfaces of the new age. Elara reached out a trembling hand toward the hovering glyphs, her fingers brushing against the cold glow of the typography.

"Hierarchy," she whispered. "Depth. The soul of the story is hidden in the spacing between the lines."

The walls hummed in response. Tonal shifts rippled through the architecture, midnight blues bleeding into soft dawn accents. It was a sophisticated, quiet interface, receding to let the prose breathe.
