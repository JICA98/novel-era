Chapter IV: The Whispering Gallery

The clockwork mechanisms of the library hummed with a quiet, rhythmic persistence that Elara had come to find comforting. It was a language of brass and oil, a mechanical heartbeat that grounded the ethereal whispers of the ancient scrolls.

"The secrets of the Nocturne are not found in the light," the Archivist had once told her, his voice like dry parchment scraping against slate.

She traced the silver filigree of the tome's spine. Every groove felt like a map leading somewhere forbidden, somewhere the stars themselves were afraid to shine.
