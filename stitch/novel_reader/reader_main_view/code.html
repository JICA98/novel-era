The Weaver of Shadows

Estimated reading time: 8 mins

The silence in the obsidian chamber was not empty; it was heavy, woven from the same lightless threads that spun the very fabric of the void. Kaelen adjusted his grip on the hilt of his blade, the cold metal a sharp reminder of the world he had left behind. Above, the ceiling vanished into an endless expanse of swirling violet mists, punctuated by stars that pulsed like dying hearts.

"You walk where even memory fears to tread," a voice echoed, though no lips moved. It was a sound like dry parchment scraping against stone. From the center of the dais, the Weaver began to manifest—a silhouette formed from the absence of light, taller than any man, its limbs elongated and fluid like ink dropped in water.

Kaelen didn't flinch. He had seen the way the shadows clung to the Weaver's fingertips, spinning stories that were never meant to be told. Each movement of the entity sent ripples through the air, distorting the architecture of the room until the floor felt like a shifting sea of glass.

"I am not here for memories," Kaelen replied, his own voice sounding thin and fragile in this cathedral of darkness. "I am here for the thread you stole. The one that belongs to the dawn."

The entity tilted its head. A thousand eyes, none of them physical, seemed to open within its shroud. "The dawn is a flicker, boy. A momentary lapse in the eternal night. Why fight for a breath when you could inherit the silence?"

With a sudden, violent surge, the shadows erupted from the floor. They didn't strike; they flowed, seeking every crack in Kaelen's resolve, whispering promises of a peace that required no effort, only surrender. He closed his eyes, centering himself not on the darkness, but on the small, burning spark within his chest—the very light the Weaver sought to extinguish.

"I choose the breath," Kaelen whispered.
