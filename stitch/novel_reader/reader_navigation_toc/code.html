Chapter 142: The Veiled Echo

The mist clung to the cobblestones like a living thing, damp and heavy with the scent of ozone. Kaelen adjusted his grip on the hilt, the cold leather familiar against his calloused palm. Behind him, the library's silhouette loomed—a monolith of forgotten wisdom in a city that had long since forgotten how to read.

Every step echoed, a rhythmic metallic click that seemed far too loud in the stillness. He had reached the threshold of the Inner Circle, a place spoken of only in hushed tones by the Digital Curators of the old world.
