About the Story

In the heart of the capital, there exists a library that only opens when the clock strikes thirteen. Elias, a disgraced curator, discovers a series of books written in the future. As he reads his own death in the ink of tomorrow, he must navigate a web of celestial politics and ancient pacts to rewrite the ending of his own tale.

The Midnight Archive is a cerebral journey through memory and fate, blending traditional gothic aesthetics with a high-stakes progression system that redefines the genre.
