A tapestry of fate woven in blood.

In the gas-lit streets of Oakhaven, shadows are not mere absences of light. They are living, breathing entities—and for Silas Vane, they are his only companions. As the last surviving Weaver, Silas has the rare ability to manipulate the silk-like threads of darkness that connect every living soul.

But power comes with a price that is paid in sanity. When a series of impossible murders leaves the city's nobility terrified, Silas is dragged from his reclusive life in the catacombs to hunt a killer who leaves no shadow behind.

As he unravels the mystery, Silas discovers a conspiracy that reaches the highest levels of the Empire. The Weaver of Shadows must decide if he will save a city that fears him, or let the darkness finally reclaim its due.

A Note from Elara

"This story is for those who find beauty in the dark corners of the world. Silas is a complex character, and Oakhaven is a city built on secrets. I hope you enjoy the descent."
