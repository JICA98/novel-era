Synopsis

In the kingdom of Aethelgard, where twilight reigns supreme, Kaelen is more than just a royal craftsman. He is the Weaver of Shadows, the last practitioner of an ancient art that turns the very absence of light into a weapon of protection.

As an encroaching darkness from the Void threatens to consume the sun forever, Kaelen must weave a tapestry of defense that spans the entire horizon. But shadows have a memory of their own, and every thread pulled from the dark risks unraveling the weaver himself.
